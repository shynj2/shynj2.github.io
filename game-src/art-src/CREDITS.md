# Credits — outside assets in Veyrath

Everything not listed here (pixel art, rigs, tiles, sound effects, music, code) is generated by the project's own
tools from `art-src/` and is original to Veyrath. Nothing is loaded from the network when the game runs.
The same list is shown in the game: pause menu → Credits (`CREDITS` in `src/js/64-credits.js`).

**Music (Round 9 §7).** Nine pieces written for Veyrath and synthesised live by the game (`src/js/21-music.js`); no recording, sample or
outside composition is used: *Preludio del Umbral* (title, intro) · *La Fragua Dormida* (workshop) · *Bosque de Ceniza* (forest) ·
*Nave Mayor* (cathedral) · *Las Máquinas Rezan* (machine halls) · *Archivo de Ramas* (repository) · *Vals del Contable* (room 499) ·
*Concierto del Guardián* (the Throne: solo violin and strings, five intensities, written for this game in Round 10) · *Lo Que Queda* (ending).

**Pictures (Round 9 §3).** The six life-stage pictures are drawn by code from the game's palette (`src/js/57-stills.js`); no photograph or outside image.

Fonts are subset at build time by `tools/fonts.py` (only the characters the game uses) and embedded in the HTML.
No images, sounds or music were downloaded.
